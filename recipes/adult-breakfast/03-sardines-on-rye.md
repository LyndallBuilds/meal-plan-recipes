## Ingredients
- 1 standard tin sardines packed in extra-virgin olive oil, drained slightly
- 1 thick slice rye or seeded sourdough
- ½ avocado, sliced
- 1 small tomato, sliced (or a few cherry tomatoes, halved)
- Large handful baby spinach or rocket
- ½ lemon
- 1 teaspoon extra-virgin olive oil
- Black pepper, flaky salt
- Optional: a few capers, or thinly sliced red onion

## Instructions
1. Toast the rye.
2. Lay avocado on the toast, mash gently with a fork. Top with sardines.
3. On the side, dress the greens with EVOO, lemon, salt, pepper. Add tomato.
4. Squeeze a little lemon over the sardines, scatter capers/red onion if using.

## Notes
- ~26 g protein
- Pantry-stable oily fish — the easiest "fish 2x/week" anchor
- Sardines in olive oil > sardines in water (the EVOO is the point)
