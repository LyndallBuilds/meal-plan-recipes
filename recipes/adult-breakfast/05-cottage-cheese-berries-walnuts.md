## Ingredients
- 1 cup (scant) 2% cottage cheese
- ½ cup mixed berries (blueberries, strawberries, raspberries)
- ¼ cup walnuts, roughly chopped (a small handful)
- 1 tablespoon ground flax (optional)
- 1 teaspoon honey or maple (optional, skip on most days)
- Pinch of cinnamon

## Instructions
1. Spoon cottage cheese into a bowl.
2. Top with berries, walnuts, flax, and cinnamon.
3. Drizzle a tiny amount of honey only if needed; the cottage cheese is satisfying on its own.
