## Ingredients
- 3–4 slices smoked salmon (about half a typical 6 oz pack)
- 2 large eggs
- 1 teaspoon extra-virgin olive oil
- 1 large handful arugula or baby spinach
- ½ avocado, sliced
- 1 slice rye or seeded sourdough bread
- ½ lemon
- Black pepper, flaky salt

## Instructions
1. Warm 1 tsp EVOO in a nonstick pan over medium-low heat. Whisk eggs with a pinch of salt and scramble gently to soft curds.
2. Toast the rye.
3. Plate: scrambled eggs, smoked salmon, sliced avocado, and a pile of arugula dressed with a small squeeze of lemon and a few drops of EVOO.
4. Crack pepper over everything and serve.

## Helper Prep Tasks
- [ ] Open the smoked salmon pack the night before so it is ready to plate.

## Notes
- ~28 g protein
- Counts toward your 2x weekly oily fish + leafy greens daily
- Skip the toast if cutting refined carbs further
