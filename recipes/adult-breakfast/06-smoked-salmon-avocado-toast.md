## Ingredients
- 3–4 slices smoked salmon (about half a typical 6 oz pack)
- 1 thick slice seeded sourdough or rye
- ½ avocado, sliced or mashed
- 1 large handful arugula or watercress
- 1 teaspoon extra-virgin olive oil
- ½ lemon
- A few thin slices of red onion (optional)
- A few capers (optional)
- Black pepper, flaky salt

## Instructions
1. Toast the bread.
2. Mash avocado over the toast with salt, pepper, and a squeeze of lemon.
3. Layer smoked salmon over the avocado.
4. Top with arugula, red onion, capers, EVOO drizzle, and another squeeze of lemon.
