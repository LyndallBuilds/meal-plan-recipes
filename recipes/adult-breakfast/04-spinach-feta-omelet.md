## Ingredients
- 3 large eggs
- 1 teaspoon extra-virgin olive oil
- 2 large handfuls baby spinach
- ¼ cup crumbled feta
- 1 small clove garlic, finely sliced (optional)
- 1 slice whole-grain sourdough or seeded rye
- Black pepper, salt
- Optional: ¼ cup berries on the side

## Instructions
1. Warm EVOO in a nonstick skillet over medium heat. Add garlic if using, then spinach. Wilt for 1–2 minutes; remove to a plate.
2. Whisk the eggs with salt and pepper. Pour into the same pan, lifting the edges so uncooked egg flows under.
3. When mostly set but still glossy on top, scatter the spinach and feta over half. Fold the omelet over and slide onto a plate.
4. Serve with toast and (optional) berries.
