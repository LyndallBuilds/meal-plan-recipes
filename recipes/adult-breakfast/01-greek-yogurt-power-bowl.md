## Ingredients
- ¾ cup 2% Greek yogurt (plain, full-fat or 2%)
- ½ cup mixed berries (blueberries + strawberries + raspberries)
- ¼ cup walnut halves, roughly broken (a small handful)
- 1 tablespoon ground flaxseed
- 1 teaspoon extra-virgin olive oil (optional finishing drizzle)
- Pinch of cinnamon
- Optional: 1 scoop whey protein OR ¼ cup cottage cheese for +15–25 g protein

## Instructions
1. Spoon the yogurt into a bowl.
2. Top with berries, walnuts, ground flax, cinnamon.
3. Optional: drizzle EVOO and stir in protein boost.

## Helper Prep Tasks
- [ ] Wash and portion 4 servings of berries into small containers (½ cup each) on Sunday for the week.
- [ ] Pre-portion 4× ¼-cup walnut servings into a single jar — no daily measuring.

## Notes
- ~32 g protein without booster, ~50 g with whey
- Hits berries + walnuts in one bowl
- Use a real plain Greek yogurt (Fage, Stonyfield, Maple Hill) — flavored versions are UPF
