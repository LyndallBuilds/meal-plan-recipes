## Ingredients
- 4 white fish fillets (about 4 oz each; cod, tilapia, sole, hake, or flounder)
- ¼ onion, chopped (optional or soft-cooked for kids)
- 1 carrot, chopped small
- 1 red bell pepper, sliced
- 1 celery stalk, chopped
- 1 clove garlic, minced
- 2 tablespoons chopped parsley (optional)
- 2 teaspoons olive oil, divided
- 4 teaspoons lemon juice, divided
- Salt and pepper for adults only (optional)

## Instructions
1. Heat oven to 400°F. Cut four large rectangles of parchment or foil; fold in half to crease, then unfold.
2. Place one fillet on each sheet. Top with chopped vegetables, garlic, and optional parsley. Keep portions plain for babies if needed.
3. Drizzle each packet with ½ teaspoon oil and 1 teaspoon lemon juice. Adults can add a pinch of salt and pepper.
4. Fold and crimp edges tightly into a half-moon seal. Transfer to a baking sheet.
5. Bake until fish reaches 145°F and flakes easily, about 10–12 minutes depending on thickness. Rest 1–2 minutes, then open carefully (steam is hot). Serve over rice or quinoa if desired.

## Baby Adaptations
- Check every flake for bones; serve soft fish and soft veggies
- Fish is an allergen—introduce per your plan
- Skip salt for under 12 months
