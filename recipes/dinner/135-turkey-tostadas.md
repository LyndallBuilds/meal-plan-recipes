## Ingredients
- 2 cups cooked turkey, cut bite-size (or 1 lb ground turkey, browned and drained)
- 2 tablespoons mild taco seasoning
- 1½ cups water
- 8 corn tortillas (family of 4–5; original uses 4)
- ½ cup low-fat refried beans
- ½ cup shredded cheddar or Monterey Jack
- 1 cup chopped tomatoes
- 1 cup shredded lettuce
- ¼ cup chopped onion (optional; mild for kids)
- Taco sauce or salsa, to taste
- Plain yogurt or mashed avocado, optional

## Instructions
1. Heat oven to 375°F. Chop tomatoes, lettuce, and optional onion.
2. In a large skillet over medium heat, combine turkey, taco seasoning, and water. Bring to a boil, then simmer about 5 minutes, stirring occasionally.
3. Place tortillas on a baking sheet. Bake 4–7 minutes until crisp.
4. Spread each tortilla with beans, top with turkey mixture and cheese. Return to oven 2–3 minutes until cheese melts.
5. Top with tomatoes, lettuce, onion, and sauce. Add yogurt or avocado if you like. Keep seasoning mild for younger kids; adults can add extra salsa at the table.

## Baby Adaptations
- Soften beans and turkey; cut tortilla into strips
- Dairy and wheat (if using flour tortillas) are allergens—introduce per your plan
- No beef, pork, or lamb
