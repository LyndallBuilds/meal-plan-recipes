# Gymkhana Coconut Curry Chicken Thighs

**Yield:** family dinner (2 adults + kids)  
**Hands-on:** about 10–15 minutes  
**Notes:** One pack boneless skinless thighs with one jar Gymkhana coconut Goan curry sauce, basmati rice, and a simple veg side (steam or roast). Sauce is labeled medium — for Leo, pull his portion earlier or stir in a spoon of plain yogurt to mellow the heat.

## Ingredients
- 1 pack boneless, skinless chicken thighs (about 1.2–1.5 pounds), cut into bite-sized pieces
- 1 jar Gymkhana Goan Curry / coconut curry simmer sauce (about 300 ml / 10 oz)
- 1–2 tablespoons neutral oil or avocado oil
- 2 tablespoons water (as on package)
- 1½ cups basmati rice (dry)
- Salt, to taste
- Vegetables for the side (pick one, about 1–1½ pounds) — any of these work well with the curry:
  - **Steam:** broccoli florets, green beans, snap peas, baby bok choy, or cauliflower florets (soft is easier for Leo)
  - **Roast (425°F, ~15–20 min):** cauliflower florets, broccoli, green beans, zucchini coins, or cubed sweet potato / delicata — toss with oil and salt; start the pan when the rice goes on
- Optional finish: cilantro or lime wedges; plain yogurt for kids

## Instructions
1. Rinse the basmati until the water runs clearer. Cook with water per package (or rice cooker) so it’s ready when the curry is done. If roasting veg, get that tray in the oven now (425°F).
2. Pat the chicken dry and season lightly with salt. Warm the oil in a wide skillet or sauté pan over medium-high heat. Add the chicken in a single layer (batch if needed) and cook until mostly opaque and lightly browned, about 6–8 minutes, stirring once or twice.
3. Pour in the Gymkhana sauce and 2 tablespoons water. Bring to a gentle boil, then reduce to a simmer. Cook until the chicken is cooked through (165°F) and the sauce is silky, about 5–8 minutes.
4. Meanwhile, steam the vegetables until tender (or soft for Leo), about 4–6 minutes — or pull the roast veg when browned and tender.
5. Serve chicken and sauce over basmati with veg on the side. Cool kids’ portions; offer yogurt to dial down heat.

## Helper Prep Tasks
- [ ] Cut chicken into bite-sized pieces; pat dry
- [ ] Measure rice; prep steamer or roast tray
- [ ] Open sauce jar; optional cilantro / yogurt bowls

## Baby Adaptations
- Sauce has chile — for twins/Leo, shred chicken into mild sauce diluted with yogurt, or serve plain chicken + rice + soft steamed veg
- Soft-steam broccoli or cauliflower for Leo rather than crisp-roast
- Check chicken is soft and fully cooked; cut small for younger eaters
- Skip cilantro garnish for picky plates
