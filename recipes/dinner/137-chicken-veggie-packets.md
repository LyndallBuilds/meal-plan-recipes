## Ingredients
- 4 boneless skinless chicken breasts (or tenders for faster cook)
- ¼ red onion, thinly sliced (optional)
- 1 carrot, julienned or thin matchsticks
- 1 red bell pepper, julienned
- 1 celery stalk, julienned
- ½ zucchini, julienned
- 1 clove garlic, minced
- 2 tablespoons chopped fresh basil (or ½ teaspoon dried)
- 2 teaspoons olive oil, divided
- 4 teaspoons lemon juice, divided
- Salt and pepper for adults only (optional)

## Instructions
1. Heat oven to 400°F. Cut four large parchment or foil rectangles; crease, then unfold.
2. Place each chicken piece in the center. Top with vegetables, garlic, and basil.
3. Drizzle each with ½ teaspoon oil and 1 teaspoon lemon juice. Season adult portions lightly if desired.
4. Seal packets tightly. Bake on a sheet pan until chicken reaches 165°F, about 20 minutes (less for thin tenders).
5. Rest 2 minutes, open carefully, and serve with rice, quinoa, or soft bread.

## Baby Adaptations
- Shred or dice chicken small; soft zucchini and pepper are good starter veggies
- No beef, pork, or lamb; keep seasoning mild
