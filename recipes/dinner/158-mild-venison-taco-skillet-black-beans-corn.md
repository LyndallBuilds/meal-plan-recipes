A family taco-night skillet like the ground-beef classic, made with venison, black beans, corn and zucchini in a mild homemade seasoning. Serve it as a build-your-own bar with tortillas or over rice. Serves 2 adults + kids. **Hands-on: about 14 minutes · Total: about 20 minutes**.

**Thaw ground venison in the fridge the day before.** Recipe assumes a 1 lb package; for 1½ lb add ½ tsp more of each spice and a splash more water.

## Ingredients
- 1 lb ground venison, thawed
- 1½ tablespoons extra-virgin olive oil
- 1 small yellow onion, finely chopped
- 1 small zucchini or 1 red bell pepper (organic), finely diced
- 1½ teaspoons ground cumin
- 1½ teaspoons sweet (not smoked or hot) paprika
- 1 teaspoon dried oregano
- ½ teaspoon garlic powder
- ¼–½ teaspoon salt
- 1 tablespoon tomato paste
- ½ cup water or low-sodium chicken broth
- 1 jar (about 14 oz) Jovial organic black beans, drained and rinsed
- 1 cup frozen organic corn
- 3 oz block Monterey Jack or mild cheddar, grated on a box grater (about ¾ cup)

### To serve (choose any)
- 8–10 small flour or corn tortillas, warmed, or 3 cups cooked rice
- 1 avocado, diced, or mashed with a squeeze of lime
- Plain whole-milk yogurt or sour cream
- Diced tomato or cucumber, lime wedges, mild salsa
- Hot sauce or sliced jalapeño for adults only

## Instructions
1. Heat oil in a large skillet over medium heat. Add onion and zucchini (or pepper) and cook 4 minutes until softened.
2. Add the venison and break it into small crumbles. Sprinkle in cumin, paprika, oregano, garlic powder and salt, then cook just until the meat is no longer pink, 3–4 minutes.
3. Stir in tomato paste, then the water, beans and frozen corn. Simmer 3–5 minutes, stirring, until saucy, heated through and the meat reaches 160°F. Add a splash more water if it looks dry, because lean venison soaks up liquid.
4. Turn off the heat, scatter the grated cheese over the top, and cover for 1–2 minutes to melt.
5. Serve from the skillet with tortillas or rice and the toppings.

## Leftovers, Freezing & Tips
- **Leftovers:** keep 4 days in the fridge. Good the next day in quesadillas or over rice for lunch.
- **Freezing:** the filling (without cheese) freezes well for up to 3 months.
- Adapted from a ground-beef taco skillet; venison swaps 1:1. The homemade spice mix replaces a store packet, so there's no added chile or fillers.

## Baby Adaptations
- For younger kids, serve the filling over soft rice or rolled in a soft tortilla cut into strips. Smash the black beans lightly with a fork, and use corn only if they handle it well (or pulse it briefly).
- Soft avocado strips are a great side. Cool to warm before serving.
- Contains dairy (cheese) and wheat (flour tortillas).
