A one-skillet take on ground-beef cottage pie: saucy venison with peas and carrots under a mashed sweet potato top, finished in the oven. The sweet potatoes cook in the microwave, so there's no peeling or boiling. Serves 2 adults + kids. **Hands-on: about 14 minutes · Total: about 45 minutes**.

**Thaw ground venison in the fridge the day before.** Recipe assumes a 1 lb package; for 1½ lb use a 12-inch skillet and add 1 more sweet potato and ½ cup more broth.

## Ingredients
- 1 lb ground venison, thawed
- 2 lb sweet potatoes (organic; about 3 medium)
- 2 tablespoons butter, divided
- 1 tablespoon extra-virgin olive oil
- 1 small yellow onion, finely chopped
- 2 cups frozen organic peas and carrots (or 1 cup each)
- 1 tablespoon all-purpose flour
- 1 tablespoon tomato paste
- 1 cup low-sodium chicken broth
- 1 teaspoon Worcestershire sauce (optional; or 1 tsp low-sodium soy sauce)
- ½ teaspoon dried thyme
- ¼ teaspoon salt, plus more for adults
- 2 oz block mild cheddar, grated on a box grater (about ½ cup; optional)

## Instructions
1. Heat the oven to 400°F. Prick the sweet potatoes all over with a fork and microwave on high for 10–12 minutes, turning once, until very soft. (Or bake whole at 400°F for 45 minutes earlier in the day.)
2. Meanwhile heat the oil and 1 tablespoon butter in a 10–12-inch oven-safe skillet over medium heat. Add the onion and cook 3 minutes until softening.
3. Add the venison, breaking it into small crumbles, and cook just until no longer pink, about 3 minutes.
4. Sprinkle in flour and stir in tomato paste for 30 seconds. Add broth, Worcestershire, thyme, salt and the frozen peas and carrots. Simmer 3–4 minutes until gravy-thick, then turn off the heat.
5. Halve the sweet potatoes, scoop the flesh into a bowl (it slips right out of the skin), and mash with a fork with the remaining 1 tablespoon butter and a pinch of salt.
6. Spoon the mash over the filling and spread it to the edges. Sprinkle the grated cheddar on top if using.
7. Bake 15–20 minutes until bubbling at the edges and the filling reaches 160°F or more. Rest 5 minutes before serving (the filling is very hot).

## Leftovers, Freezing & Tips
- **Leftovers:** keep 4 days in the fridge; reheat covered to 165°F.
- **Freezing:** cool, then freeze in portions or in a foil pan for up to 3 months. Thaw overnight and bake covered at 375°F for 25–30 minutes.
- White potatoes work too: use 2 lb Yukon Golds, microwave the same way, and mash with butter and a splash of milk.
- Adapted from a ground-beef cottage pie; venison swaps 1:1.

## Baby Adaptations
- Serve younger kids a scoop with soft mash on top; the peas can be lightly smashed with a fork. Let it cool well, because the filling stays hot longer than the top.
- Keep salt light, and skip Worcestershire on their portion if you like (it's only a teaspoon in the whole pan).
- Contains dairy (butter, cheddar) and wheat (flour; use cornstarch for gluten-free).
