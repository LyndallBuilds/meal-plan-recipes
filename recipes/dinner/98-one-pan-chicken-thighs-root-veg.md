## Ingredients
- 4–6 bone-in skinless chicken thighs, or 4 large boneless skinless thighs, trimmed
- 1 pound small potatoes, halved or quartered
- 2–3 large carrots, peeled and cut in chunks
- 1 red onion, cut in wedges
- 3 tablespoons extra-virgin olive oil
- 1 teaspoon sweet paprika (mild) or ½ teaspoon each dried thyme and garlic powder
- Salt and pepper for adults; minimal salt for shared pieces for kids

## Instructions
1. Heat oven to 425°F. Lightly oil a large rimmed sheet pan.
2. Toss vegetables with most of the oil, paprika or herbs, and a light hand of salt. Spread in an even layer and nestle chicken in between.
3. Drizzle chicken with remaining oil; add a very light dusting of the same seasonings on the chicken.
4. Roast 35–40 minutes, turning the vegetables once, until chicken reaches 165°F in the thickest part and vegetables are tender.
5. Let rest 5 minutes. Shred or chop chicken for little ones; offer soft potato and carrot in appropriate sizes.

## Helper Prep Tasks
- [ ] **Sheet-pan “oven-ready” goal:** line and lightly oil a large rimmed sheet pan. Wash, peel, and cut the potatoes, carrots, and onion to size; pat dry, trim, and season the chicken.
- [ ] In a very large bowl, toss the vegetables and chicken with the oil, paprika, and salt as written, then **turn the whole thing out in one even layer: vegetables spread and chicken nestled between pieces** (single layer, not stacked). The cook should only have to preheat the oven, slide the pan in, and turn the veg once mid-roast.
- [ ] If you must break between prep and dinner, **cover the fully loaded sheet** (foil) and refrigerate up to 24 hours, or keep the oiled, seasoned mixture in a bowl and **spread it onto the lined pan in one step** at bake time.

## Baby Adaptations
- Ensure pieces are very soft; avoid crispy edges for young babies if they are too firm
- Poultry: cook through; cut against the grain for toddlers
