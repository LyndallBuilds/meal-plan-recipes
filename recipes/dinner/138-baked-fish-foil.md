## Ingredients
- Nonstick cooking spray or a light brush of oil
- 1 pound mild white fish fillets (tilapia, trout, or similar)
- 1 small onion, thinly sliced (optional for kids; soft-cook or omit)
- 2 teaspoons olive or vegetable oil
- Pinch lemon pepper or squeeze of lemon (optional)
- Salt and black pepper for adults only (optional)

## Instructions
1. Heat oven to 350°F. Lay a ~12-inch sheet of foil on the counter; mist with spray or brush lightly with oil.
2. Place fillets in the center (skin side down if present). Spread onion, oil, and optional lemon pepper on top. Keep baby portions plain if needed.
3. Fold foil into a sealed pouch. Set on a baking sheet.
4. Bake 15–20 minutes until fish flakes easily with a fork. Divide into portions and serve with steamed veggies or rice.

## Baby Adaptations
- Flake carefully and check for bones
- Fish allergen—introduce per your plan; skip salt for babies
