## Ingredients
- 1 block (14–16 oz) extra-firm tofu, pressed 15+ minutes, cubed
- 1 tablespoon neutral oil
- 1 can (14 oz) light coconut milk, divided
- 1 small shallot, minced (or 2 tablespoons minced leek, mostly white part, mild)
- 1 clove garlic, minced
- 1 cup jasmine or long-grain rice, rinsed
- Zest and juice of 1 lime, plus extra wedges
- 1–2 cups frozen peas or very fine chopped green beans, thawed or brief-steamed
- 1 handful chopped fresh cilantro (optional; can omit for picky)

## Instructions
1. Press tofu; pat very dry. Pan-fry cubes in 1 teaspoon oil in a nonstick pan over medium-high until golden on most sides, about 8–10 minutes, turning gently. Set aside.
2. In a pot, warm remaining oil. Cook shallot 2–3 minutes; add garlic 30 seconds. Add rice, stir 1 minute.
3. Add 1 cup coconut milk plus enough water to match your rice’s usual liquid (measure per package, replacing part with coconut). Bring to a boil, then simmer covered until liquid is absorbed and rice is tender, about 15–18 minutes.
4. Gently fold in peas, remaining coconut milk to taste, lime zest, and half the lime juice. Add tofu. Warm through; add cilantro if using.
5. Serve with lime wedges. Keep a very mild portion for babies before adding much lime or cilantro to the whole pot.

## Helper Prep Tasks
- [ ] Press and cube tofu; store in a container
- [ ] Mince shallot and garlic; store separately
- [ ] Thaw peas or prep green beans

## Baby Adaptations
- Soy and coconut are allergens—per your plan
- Rice should be very soft; squish tofu for younger eaters
- Spices are mild; increase lime at the table for adults
