## Ingredients
- 1¼–1½ pounds cod (or other mild white fish) fillets, patted dry
- 1 large head broccoli, cut into small florets
- 3 tablespoons extra-virgin olive oil, divided
- 1 lemon, zest and juice
- 1 teaspoon garlic powder or 2 cloves minced garlic
- 1–2 teaspoons chopped fresh dill or parsley, or ½ teaspoon dried
- ¼ teaspoon each salt and pepper for adults (keep fish plain for babies)

## Instructions
1. Heat oven to 425°F. Line a large sheet pan with parchment.
2. Toss broccoli with 1–2 tablespoons oil, a pinch of salt for adults, and half the lemon juice. Spread on the pan. Roast 8 minutes.
3. While broccoli starts, place fish on the clear side of the pan or a second small pan. Drizzle with remaining oil, lemon zest, garlic, and herbs. Add a little lemon juice.
4. Return pan to the oven 8–10 minutes more until fish flakes easily and broccoli is tender. Let rest 2–3 minutes.
5. For babies, offer a soft flake of fish and very soft broccoli with little or no added salt. Adults can add salt, pepper, and extra lemon at the table.

## Helper Prep Tasks
- [ ] **Sheet-pan “oven-ready” goal:** line a large sheet pan (parchment or foil). This recipe bakes the broccoli a few minutes first, then adds the fish—helper can still get the pan **as far as the first bake.**
- [ ] Wash and break down the broccoli, toss with the first part of the oil, salt, and lemon, and **spread in an even layer on the pan**; cover and refrigerate if holding. Zest and juice the lemon, mince garlic, prep oil and dried herbs, pat the fish **dry, portioned, and seasoned on a second plate** so the cook only opens the oven, adds fish, and finishes the timer (no more knife work on the line).
- [ ] If you prefer, leave broccoli **oiled in a container** and the **pan lined but empty**; then spread broccoli just before the first roast—still a single “place on pan” action at cook time.

## Baby Adaptations
- Fish and broccoli are top allergens in some cases—serve per your introduction plan
- Check all fish for bones; flake to check temperature
- No pork, beef, or lamb; mild seasoning throughout
