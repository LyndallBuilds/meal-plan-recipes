An original recipe written for this household. Herby turkey patties with grated zucchini to keep them juicy roast on one pan with sweet potato wedges and broccoli. Serves 2 adults + kids. **Hands-on: about 12 minutes · Total: about 40 minutes.**

**Whole30 check:** compliant. There's no breadcrumb or egg binder (the zucchini and a spoonful of compatible mustard hold the patties together), no dairy and no sugar. If you serve ketchup, it must be an unsweetened compatible brand.

## Ingredients
- 1½ lb ground turkey (93% lean; organic)
- 1 small zucchini (organic), coarsely grated and squeezed dry in a towel (about ¾ cup)
- 2 tablespoons chopped fresh parsley or chives (or 2 teaspoons dried parsley)
- 1 tablespoon Dijon mustard (compatible, no sugar)
- 1 teaspoon garlic powder
- 1 teaspoon onion powder
- ½ teaspoon dried oregano
- ¾ teaspoon kosher salt, divided
- 1½ lb sweet potatoes (organic; about 2 large), scrubbed and cut into ½-inch wedges
- 1 large head broccoli (or 12 oz pre-cut florets), cut into bite-size florets
- 3 tablespoons avocado oil or olive oil, divided
- To serve: lettuce leaves for wraps, sliced tomato, avocado, compatible mayo or unsweetened ketchup

## Instructions
1. Heat the oven to 425°F with racks in the upper and lower thirds. Line two rimmed sheet pans with parchment or oil them lightly.
2. On pan 1, toss the sweet potato wedges with 2 tablespoons oil and ¼ teaspoon salt and spread them out. Roast on the lower rack for 10 minutes while you make the patties.
3. In a bowl, gently mix the turkey, squeezed zucchini, herbs, mustard, garlic powder, onion powder, oregano and ½ teaspoon salt with your hands until just combined. Form 6 patties about ¾ inch thick (wet your hands to stop sticking), plus 2–3 mini patties for younger kids if you like.
4. Put the patties on one side of pan 2. Toss the broccoli with the remaining 1 tablespoon oil right on the other side of the pan.
5. Put pan 2 on the upper rack and move the sweet potatoes up beside it or keep them below. Roast 18–20 minutes, flipping the patties once if you remember (not essential), until they reach 165°F in the center and the broccoli is browned at the edges. Mini patties take about 12–14 minutes.
6. Serve the burgers in lettuce wraps or on the plate with the sweet potatoes, broccoli and toppings.

## Household Tweaks
- **Mild:** no chile anywhere. Adults can add compatible hot sauce.
- **Kid portions:** mini patties and wedges for dipping in a little compatible mayo or unsweetened ketchup.
- **Scaling:** for bigger appetites or lunches, use 2 lb turkey with a slightly larger zucchini and 1 tsp salt in the patties.

## Leftovers
- Keeps 4 days in the fridge; reheat at 375°F for 8–10 minutes or in the microwave.
- Cooked patties freeze well for up to 3 months. You can also freeze raw patties between parchment, then bake from frozen at 425°F for 25–28 minutes until they reach 165°F.

## Baby Adaptations
- **Younger kids:** cut the patties into thin strips or small pieces and check that they're cooked through (165°F). Peel the roasted sweet potato and serve it in soft wedges or lightly mashed. Steam-soft broccoli florets are good for them; give them the softer, less crispy pieces.
- Salt is moderate (about ¾ tsp across the whole pan); skip extra salt and ketchup on their plates.
- **Older kids:** a mini burger in a lettuce wrap with sweet potato wedges.
- Contains mustard.
