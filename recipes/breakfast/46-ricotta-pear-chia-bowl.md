## Ingredients
- ½ cup full-fat whole milk ricotta
- ½ ripe pear, peeled and grated or very finely chopped
- 1–2 teaspoons chia seeds (optional)
- 1 pinch ground cinnamon
- 1 small squeeze of lemon (optional, to keep pear bright)
- 1–2 whole grain toast soldiers (optional, for scooping)

## Instructions
1. Stir ricotta in a small bowl until smooth. Fold in pear, optional chia, cinnamon, and optional lemon.
2. Serve at room temperature or slightly chilled, with or without toast cut into age-appropriate shapes.

## Helper Prep Tasks
- [ ] Grate pear and store with a bit of lemon in airtight container
- [ ] Pre-portion ricotta if helpful

## Baby Adaptations
- Use smooth ricotta; ensure pear is soft enough for your child’s age
- Contains dairy; introduce per your allergen schedule
