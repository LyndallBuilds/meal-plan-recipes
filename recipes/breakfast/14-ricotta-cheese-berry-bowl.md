## Ingredients
- ¾–1 cup whole-milk ricotta (or silken tofu as a dairy-free swap)
- ¼–½ cup fresh or thawed berries (blueberries, raspberries, and/or strawberries)
- Optional: toast strips or a thin rice cake for dipping
- Optional: pinch of ground almond or cinnamon

## Instructions
1. Spoon ricotta into bowls.
2. Rinse berries; halve or mash larger ones for younger eaters. Scatter over ricotta (or serve on the side).
3. Add optional toast strips and a light sprinkle of almond or cinnamon.

## Helper Prep Tasks
- [ ] Portion ricotta into containers
- [ ] Wash and cut berries (best within a day)

## Baby Adaptations
- Dairy (or soy if using tofu) and optional tree nut/wheat: introduce per your plan
- Mash berries and ricotta together for easier scooping
