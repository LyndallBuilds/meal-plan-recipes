## Ingredients
- ¾–1 cup plain unsweetened whole-milk yogurt (or fortified plant yogurt)
- 1 tablespoon smooth almond butter
- Optional: grated apple or soft fruit on top
- Optional: pinch of cinnamon

## Instructions
1. Whisk yogurt and almond butter in a bowl until smooth and lump-free. Loosen with a spoonful more yogurt if needed.
2. Top with grated apple or fruit and a pinch of cinnamon if using.
3. Serve cool.

## Helper Prep Tasks
- [ ] Mix yogurt + almond butter ahead (keeps 2–3 days in the fridge; stir before serving)
- [ ] Wash and grate apple same day

## Baby Adaptations
- Dairy and tree-nut (almond) allergens: introduce per your plan
- Use a thin, smooth almond butter with no added sugar or salt for babies
