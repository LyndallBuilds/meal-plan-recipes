## Ingredients
- 1 cup rolled oats
- 1 cup unsweetened whole milk or fortified plant milk
- ½ cup full-fat plain Greek yogurt (optional, for extra creaminess)
- 1 small apple, finely diced or grated
- 1 tablespoon chia seeds (optional)
- ½ teaspoon ground cinnamon
- 1–2 teaspoons maple syrup (optional, for older kids and adults)

## Instructions
1. The night before, combine oats, milk, optional yogurt, apple, optional chia, and cinnamon in a container with a tight lid. Stir well.
2. Refrigerate 8 hours or overnight.
3. In the morning, stir; thin with a splash of milk if needed. Add maple syrup to taste for those who are ready for a little sweetness. Serve cool or let sit at room temperature 10–15 minutes if the family prefers it less cold.

## Helper Prep Tasks
- [ ] Mix overnight oats in portions (store in individual jars in fridge)
- [ ] Dice or grate apple

## Baby Adaptations
- For younger babies, use softer rolled oats; omit maple syrup; ensure apple is very soft or stewed
- Check temperature before serving; cold oats are fine if tolerated
- Contains dairy if using milk/yogurt—introduce allergens per your family’s plan
