## Ingredients
- ¾–1 cup small-curd or whipped cottage cheese
- ½ cup fresh berries, diced mango, or finely chopped soft fruit
- 1 small drizzle of olive oil (optional) or a sprinkle of ground flaxseed (optional)
- 1 pinch cinnamon (optional)

## Instructions
1. Rinse and dry fruit. Cut into very small, soft pieces for younger eaters.
2. Spoon cottage cheese into bowls, top with fruit, optional oil or flax, and optional cinnamon.
3. Stir gently for a younger toddler or keep toppings separate for picky eaters.

## Helper Prep Tasks
- [ ] Wash and chop soft fruit (store in fridge)

## Baby Adaptations
- Choose low-sodium cottage cheese for babies; mash larger curds if needed
- Dairy allergen: introduce per your plan
