## Ingredients
- ½ cup rolled oats (or instant oats)
- ½ cup milk of choice + ½ cup water (or 1 cup milk)
- 1 small apple, peeled and finely diced or grated
- ½ teaspoon ground cinnamon
- Optional: pinch of ground walnut or a drizzle of maple for older kids/adults

## Instructions
1. In a small pot, bring milk/water and diced apple to a gentle simmer.
2. Stir in oats and cinnamon. Cook, stirring, until thick and creamy, about 5–8 minutes for rolled oats (less for instant).
3. Cool to a comfortable temperature. Top with optional walnut or maple.
4. For a softer apple, simmer the apple a few minutes before adding oats.

## Helper Prep Tasks
- [ ] Make a bigger batch; refrigerate up to 3–4 days and reheat with a splash of milk
- [ ] Dice or grate apple

## Baby Adaptations
- Dairy and optional tree nut: introduce per your plan
- Cook until very soft; mash apple into the oats; skip maple and salt for babies
