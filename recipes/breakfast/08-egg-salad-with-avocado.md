## Ingredients
- 3 eggs
- ½–1 ripe avocado
- Pinch of onion powder or black pepper (optional)
- Optional: sesame or hemp seeds on top
- Optional side: extra avocado spears or soft fruit

## Instructions
1. Hard-boil the eggs: lower into boiling water, cook 10–12 minutes, then cool in ice water and peel.
2. Mash eggs with avocado until mostly smooth (a little texture is fine). Season lightly if desired.
3. Scoop into bowls. Sprinkle optional seeds. Serve with fruit or avocado spears if you like.

## Helper Prep Tasks
- [ ] Boil eggs ahead (keep in shell up to 1 week in the fridge)
- [ ] Mash with avocado just before serving for best color (or store mixed salad up to 2 days)

## Baby Adaptations
- Egg (and sesame if used) allergens: introduce per your plan
- Mash smooth for younger babies; offer soft pieces for finger-feeding toddlers
