## Ingredients
- 2 eggs, scrambled soft
- ½ cup shredded cheddar or Colby-Jack
- 4 flour tortillas (6–8 inch)
- Mild salsa, optional (about 4 tablespoons total)

## Instructions
1. Scramble eggs until just set. Warm tortillas if needed.
2. Put about 2 tablespoons cheese and ¼ of the eggs on each tortilla.
3. Microwave 2 at a time on high 30–45 seconds until cheese melts, or warm in a skillet. Fold and top with optional mild salsa.
4. Cut into wedges or strips for little hands.

## Baby Adaptations
- Soft scramble; cut small pieces
- Egg, dairy, and wheat allergens—introduce per your plan
