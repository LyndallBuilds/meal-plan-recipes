## Ingredients
- ¾–1 cup plain unsweetened whole-milk yogurt (or fortified plant yogurt)
- 1 tablespoon smooth peanut butter
- Optional: banana spears or slices
- Optional: pinch of ground peanut or crushed peanuts for older kids

## Instructions
1. Whisk yogurt and peanut butter until completely smooth.
2. Serve with banana on the side or stirred in.
3. For older kids, a light sprinkle of crushed peanut is optional.

## Helper Prep Tasks
- [ ] Mix yogurt + peanut butter ahead (keeps 2–3 days refrigerated)

## Baby Adaptations
- Dairy and peanut allergens: introduce per your plan
- Choose smooth, unsweetened peanut butter; thin with yogurt so it is not sticky
