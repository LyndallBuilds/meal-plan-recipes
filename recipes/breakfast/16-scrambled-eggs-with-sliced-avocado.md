## Ingredients
- 2 eggs
- 1 teaspoon olive oil or butter
- ½ ripe avocado, cut into spears or slices
- Pinch of salt (optional, for older kids and adults)
- Optional: sesame seeds for rolling avocado, or fruit on the side

## Instructions
1. Crack eggs into a bowl and whisk until combined.
2. Warm oil in a small nonstick skillet over medium heat. Pour in eggs and stir gently until softly set, about 3–5 minutes. Remove from heat.
3. Scoop scrambled eggs into bowls. Serve with avocado spears alongside (or mashed into the eggs for younger eaters).
4. Cool to a comfortable temperature before serving kids.

## Helper Prep Tasks
- [ ] Scramble eggs fresh when possible (leftovers keep 1 day in the fridge)
- [ ] Cut avocado just before serving

## Baby Adaptations
- Egg allergen: serve only after eggs have been introduced safely
- Soft scramble and ripe avocado are easy to mash or offer as finger food
- Skip added salt for babies
