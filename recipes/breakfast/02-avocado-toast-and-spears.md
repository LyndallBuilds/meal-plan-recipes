## Ingredients
- 1 slice soft bread or a thin rice cake
- ½ ripe avocado
- Squeeze of lime or lemon (optional)
- Drizzle of olive oil (optional)
- Pinch of sesame seeds (optional)
- Optional side: tomato wedges

## Instructions
1. Lightly toast the bread if desired.
2. Mash some of the avocado onto the toast; cut the rest into spears for finger food.
3. Add optional lime/lemon, olive oil, and sesame seeds.
4. Cut toast into strips for little hands, or leave whole for older kids and adults.

## Helper Prep Tasks
- [ ] Toast and cut bread into strips (best same day)
- [ ] Mash avocado fresh — it browns quickly

## Baby Adaptations
- Wheat and sesame allergens: introduce per your plan
- Soft bread and ripe avocado work well as finger food
- For younger babies, thinly spread mashed avocado on soft toast strips
