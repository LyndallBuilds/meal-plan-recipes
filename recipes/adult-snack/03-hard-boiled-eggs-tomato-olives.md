## Ingredients
- 2 hard-boiled eggs (cooked and chilled)
- 1 small tomato, sliced, OR a handful cherry tomatoes
- 8–10 kalamata olives
- ¼ cup crumbled feta OR a matchbox-sized piece (~1 oz) of cheddar (optional, +6 g protein)
- ½ teaspoon extra-virgin olive oil
- Flaky salt, black pepper, dried oregano

## Instructions
1. Halve the eggs.
2. Plate with tomatoes, olives, optional cheese.
3. Salt, pepper, oregano, EVOO drizzle.

## Helper Prep Tasks
- [ ] Boil 6–8 eggs Sunday so the eggs are pre-made for snacks all week.

## Notes
- ~18 g protein with cheese, ~12 without
- Quick, portable, no cooking on the day
