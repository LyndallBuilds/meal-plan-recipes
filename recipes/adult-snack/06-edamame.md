## Ingredients
- 1 cup shelled edamame (frozen is fine — buy organic if you can)
- Flaky sea salt
- Optional: chili flakes, a splash of soy sauce, or sesame seeds

## Instructions
1. Steam or microwave edamame from frozen until hot through, about 3–5 minutes.
2. Toss with flaky salt and any optional seasoning.

## Notes
- ~17 g protein per cup shelled
- Cheap, plant-based, fiber-rich
- Buy in pods for a more satisfying eat-with-hands snack
