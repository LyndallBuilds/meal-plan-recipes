## Ingredients
- 1 scoop whey protein isolate (vanilla or unflavored, ~25 g protein per scoop)
- 250 ml whole or 2% milk (or unsweetened oat milk for lower saturated fat)
- ½ cup frozen berries (blueberries + strawberries)
- 1 tablespoon ground flax or chia
- A few ice cubes

## Instructions
1. Blend everything for 30 seconds until smooth.

## Notes
- ~30 g protein
- Fastest option for a tight day — also covers berries
- Pick a whey with <5 ingredients on the label and minimal sweeteners
