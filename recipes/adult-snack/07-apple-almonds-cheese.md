## Ingredients
- 1 medium apple, sliced
- ¼ cup almonds (a small handful)
- 1 oz sharp cheddar or aged gouda (a matchbox-sized piece)

## Instructions
1. Slice apple, plate with almonds and cheese.

## Notes
- ~14 g protein
- Lower-protein option — if it's your only snack, pair with a small Greek yogurt
- Hard cheese in a portion this size is reasonable; the saturated-fat watch is for cheese-as-meal
