## Ingredients
- 1 standard tin tuna in extra-virgin olive oil, drained
- 4–6 whole-grain seeded crackers
- 1 teaspoon Dijon mustard
- ½ lemon
- A handful arugula
- Black pepper

## Instructions
1. Mix the tuna with Dijon and a squeeze of lemon.
2. Top crackers with arugula, then tuna mixture. Crack pepper.

## Notes
- ~26 g protein
- Tuna is *not* an oily fish like salmon/sardines/mackerel; rotate as a different snack option
- Albacore is mercury-heavier — use skipjack/light tuna for frequent eating
