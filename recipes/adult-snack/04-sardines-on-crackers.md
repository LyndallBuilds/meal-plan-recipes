## Ingredients
- 1 standard tin sardines in extra-virgin olive oil
- 4–6 whole-grain seeded crackers (Wasa, Mary's Gone, Akmak, etc.)
- ½ lemon
- A few thin slices of red onion (optional)
- Black pepper

## Instructions
1. Open the tin, lay sardines on the crackers.
2. Squeeze lemon over the top, scatter red onion if using, crack pepper.

## Notes
- ~25 g protein
- Pantry-stable — your easiest oily fish anchor
- Pick crackers with <5 ingredients (whole grain + seeds + salt)
