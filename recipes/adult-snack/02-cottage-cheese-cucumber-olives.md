## Ingredients
- 1 cup (scant) 2% cottage cheese
- ½ cucumber, sliced
- 8–10 kalamata or Castelvetrano olives
- 1 teaspoon extra-virgin olive oil
- Black pepper, dried oregano

## Instructions
1. Spoon cottage cheese into a small bowl.
2. Arrange cucumber slices and olives around it.
3. Drizzle EVOO, crack pepper, sprinkle a little oregano.

## Notes
- ~24 g protein
- Savory, salty, satisfying — good late-afternoon option
