## Ingredients
- ½ cup hummus (look for short-ingredient brands or homemade — chickpeas, tahini, lemon, garlic, EVOO, salt)
- 1 cup raw vegetables: carrots, cucumber, bell pepper strips, cherry tomatoes
- 8–10 kalamata or Castelvetrano olives
- 1 hard-boiled egg (optional, +6 g protein)
- 1 teaspoon extra-virgin olive oil drizzled over the hummus
- Pinch of paprika or za'atar

## Instructions
1. Spread hummus on a small plate, drizzle EVOO, sprinkle paprika or za'atar.
2. Arrange vegetables, olives, and the optional egg around it.

## Notes
- ~12 g protein (~18 g with the egg)
- Easy to add a few extras (cheese cubes, smoked turkey if you do it) for more protein
- Hummus = legumes daily anchor
