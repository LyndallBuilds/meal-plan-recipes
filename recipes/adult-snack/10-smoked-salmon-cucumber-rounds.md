## Ingredients
- 3–4 slices smoked salmon (about half a typical 6 oz pack)
- ½ cucumber, sliced into thick rounds
- 2 tablespoons cream cheese, Boursin, or labneh
- Fresh dill or chives
- A few capers
- Black pepper, lemon zest

## Instructions
1. Lay cucumber rounds on a plate, top each with a small smear of cream cheese.
2. Lay smoked salmon on top, finish with dill, capers, lemon zest, pepper.

## Notes
- ~20 g protein
- Counts toward oily fish 2x/week
- Replace cream cheese with labneh for a less saturated-fat option
