## Ingredients
- 1 palm-sized chicken breast (~6 oz, boneless and skinless)
- 2 large handfuls mixed leafy greens (rocket, spinach, romaine)
- ½ cucumber, sliced
- 1 small tomato, sliced (or cherry tomatoes)
- ¼ red onion, very thinly sliced
- 1 small avocado (optional)
- 2 tablespoons extra-virgin olive oil, divided
- 2 teaspoons lemon juice
- ½ teaspoon Dijon mustard
- 1 small garlic clove, finely grated
- Salt, pepper, dried oregano

## Instructions
1. Pound chicken breast to even thickness. Toss with 1 tsp EVOO, salt, pepper, oregano.
2. Sear in a hot pan 4–5 minutes per side until 165°F internal. Rest 3 minutes, slice.
3. Whisk remaining EVOO with lemon, Dijon, garlic, salt, pepper in a bowl.
4. Toss greens, cucumber, tomato, red onion in the dressing. Top with sliced chicken and avocado.
