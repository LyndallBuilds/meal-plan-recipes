## Ingredients
- 1 palm-sized chicken breast (~6 oz, boneless and skinless)
- 1 teaspoon extra-virgin olive oil
- ½ lemon
- 1 small clove garlic, grated (optional)
- Salt, pepper, dried oregano or thyme

## Instructions
1. Pound the breast to even thickness if needed. Toss with EVOO, lemon zest, garlic, salt, pepper, and herbs.
2. Sear in a hot pan or grill 4–5 minutes per side until 165°F internal.
3. Rest 3 minutes, slice, squeeze lemon, serve alongside the kids' dinner.

## Helper Prep Tasks
- [ ] Cook 3–4 breasts at once on Sunday/Wednesday — pre-cooked chicken is the cheat code for hitting protein.

## Notes
- ~40 g protein per palm-sized cooked breast
- Use this on nights when the kids' dinner is low-protein for you (pasta, frittata, bean-only meals)
