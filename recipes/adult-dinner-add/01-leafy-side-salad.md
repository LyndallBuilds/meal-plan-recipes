## Ingredients
- 2 large handfuls mixed leafy greens (rocket, baby spinach, kale, romaine, watercress)
- ½ cucumber, sliced
- 1 small tomato or a handful cherry tomatoes
- ¼ red onion, very thinly sliced
- 1 tablespoon extra-virgin olive oil
- 2 teaspoons lemon juice OR red wine vinegar
- ½ teaspoon Dijon mustard
- 1 small clove garlic, finely grated (optional)
- Flaky salt, black pepper
- Optional: ¼ cup crumbled feta, a few olives, walnuts, or shaved parmesan

## Instructions
1. Whisk EVOO, lemon, Dijon, garlic, salt, pepper in the bottom of the bowl.
2. Add greens and other vegetables, toss to coat.
3. Add any optional toppings just before serving.

## Helper Prep Tasks
- [ ] Wash and dry a tub of mixed greens Sunday — keeps for 4–5 days in a paper-towel-lined container.
- [ ] Make a jar of dressing (4× recipe) so it is ready every night.

## Notes
- ~3 g protein on its own — pair with the kids' dinner protein
- This is your daily leafy-greens anchor — make it the default beside every dinner
- Vary the greens through the week so it never feels routine
