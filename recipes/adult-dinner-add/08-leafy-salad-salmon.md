## Ingredients
- 1 palm-sized salmon fillet (~6 oz, skin on)
- 2 large handfuls mixed leafy greens
- ½ cucumber, sliced
- ¼ red onion, thinly sliced
- 1 small tomato or a handful cherry tomatoes
- ½ avocado, sliced (optional)
- 2 tablespoons extra-virgin olive oil
- 2 teaspoons lemon juice
- ½ teaspoon Dijon
- Salt, pepper, dill or parsley

## Instructions
1. Pat salmon dry, rub with 1 tsp EVOO, salt, pepper. Sear skin-down in a hot pan 4–5 minutes; flip and cook 2–3 more minutes (medium). Rest 3 minutes, flake.
2. Whisk remaining EVOO with lemon, Dijon, salt, pepper.
3. Toss greens, cucumber, red onion, tomato in dressing. Top with flaked salmon and avocado. Finish with herbs.

## Notes
- ~33 g protein
- Counts toward oily fish 2x/week + leafy greens
- Use cold leftover salmon (cook 2 fillets the night before) to make this a no-cook assembly
