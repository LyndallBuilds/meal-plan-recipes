## Ingredients
- 1 cup shelled edamame (frozen, organic if possible)
- ½ teaspoon flaky sea salt
- Optional: chili flakes, sesame seeds, splash of soy

## Instructions
1. Steam or microwave from frozen until hot.
2. Toss with salt and any optional seasoning.

## Notes
- ~17 g protein
- Quick legume side — pairs especially well with Asian-leaning dinners (the noodles, fried-rice, bibimbap nights)
