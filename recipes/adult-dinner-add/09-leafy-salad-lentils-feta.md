## Ingredients
- 1 cup cooked green or French (Puy) lentils — pre-cooked tin/pouch is fine, drained
- 2 large handfuls mixed leafy greens
- ½ cucumber, sliced
- 1 small tomato or a handful cherry tomatoes
- ¼ red onion, very thinly sliced
- ¼ cup crumbled feta
- 2 tablespoons extra-virgin olive oil
- 2 teaspoons red wine vinegar OR lemon juice
- ½ teaspoon Dijon mustard
- Small handful chopped parsley or mint
- Salt, pepper

## Instructions
1. Whisk EVOO, vinegar, Dijon, salt, pepper at the bottom of a bowl.
2. Add greens, lentils, cucumber, tomato, red onion, herbs. Toss.
3. Top with crumbled feta and crack pepper.
