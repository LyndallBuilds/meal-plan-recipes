## Ingredients
- 1 palm-sized salmon fillet (~6 oz, skin on)
- 1 teaspoon extra-virgin olive oil
- ½ lemon
- Salt, pepper

## Instructions
1. Pat the fillet dry. Rub with EVOO, salt, pepper.
2. Cook skin-side down in a hot pan over medium-high heat for 4–5 minutes until skin is crisp; flip and cook 2–3 more minutes (medium).
3. Squeeze lemon, serve.

## Notes
- ~33 g protein per palm-sized fillet
- Counts toward oily fish 2x/week — use on a night when the kids' dinner doesn't have fish
- Buy 2 fillets, cook both, eat one cold over salad the next day
