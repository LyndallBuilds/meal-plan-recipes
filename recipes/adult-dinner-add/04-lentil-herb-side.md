## Ingredients
- 1 cup cooked green or French (Puy) lentils — pre-cooked tin/pouch is fine, drained
- 1 tablespoon extra-virgin olive oil
- 2 teaspoons red wine vinegar or lemon juice
- ½ teaspoon Dijon mustard
- Small handful chopped parsley, mint, or dill
- 1 small shallot, very finely diced (optional)
- Salt, pepper

## Instructions
1. Whisk EVOO, vinegar, Dijon, salt, pepper in a bowl.
2. Toss lentils, herbs, shallot in the dressing. Taste and adjust.

## Helper Prep Tasks
- [ ] Cook a big pot of lentils Sunday (or buy 2–3 pouches) — uses across snacks and dinners.

## Notes
- ~18 g protein per cup
- Big fiber + plant-protein hit for nights when the kids' dinner is fish/chicken without much carb depth
- Add cherry tomatoes, feta, or roasted veg to make it heartier
