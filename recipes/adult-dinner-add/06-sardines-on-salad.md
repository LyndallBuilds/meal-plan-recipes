## Ingredients
- 1 standard tin sardines in extra-virgin olive oil
- 2 large handfuls mixed greens
- ½ cucumber, sliced
- 1 small tomato, sliced (or cherry tomatoes)
- ¼ red onion, thinly sliced
- ½ lemon
- 1 tablespoon extra-virgin olive oil
- ½ teaspoon Dijon
- A few olives or capers (optional)
- Salt, pepper

## Instructions
1. Whisk EVOO, lemon, Dijon, salt, pepper in the bottom of the bowl.
2. Add greens, cucumber, tomato, red onion. Toss.
3. Lay sardines on top, scatter olives or capers, finish with pepper.

## Notes
- ~25 g protein
- Hits leafy greens + oily fish + EVOO in a single dish — your "fish 2x/week" insurance plan
- Works as a bigger-portion lunch swap if you skip the meal service
