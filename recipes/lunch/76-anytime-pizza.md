## Ingredients
- 2 English muffins, split (or ¼ baguette split), lightly toasted
- ½ cup pizza sauce or mild marinara
- ½ cup shredded part-skim mozzarella or cheddar
- ¼ cup chopped green pepper (optional)
- ¼ cup sliced mushrooms, fresh or canned (optional)
- Other mild veggie toppings as desired
- Pinch Italian seasoning (optional)

## Instructions
1. Toast muffin halves or bread until lightly brown.
2. Top with sauce, optional veggies, and cheese. Sprinkle Italian seasoning if using.
3. Bake at 350°F (or toaster oven) until cheese melts, about 5–8 minutes. Cool slightly before serving to kids.

## Baby Adaptations
- Cut into small strips; soft veggies only
- Wheat and dairy allergens—introduce per your plan
