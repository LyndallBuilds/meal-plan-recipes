## Ingredients
- ¾ cup chunky salsa or pico de gallo, drained of excess liquid
- 1 can (15.5 oz) low-sodium black beans, drained and rinsed
- 2 cups shredded Colby-Jack or cheddar (use less for lighter plates)
- 2 tablespoons finely chopped cilantro (optional)
- 4 flour tortillas (8-inch)
- ½ teaspoon olive oil

## Instructions
1. Drain salsa well. Mix salsa solids, black beans, cheese, and optional cilantro in a bowl.
2. Divide mixture over half of each tortilla (~½ cup each). Fold in half.
3. Heat a large skillet or griddle over medium-high; brush with oil. Cook quesadillas, flipping once, until golden and cheese melts, about 5 minutes total.
4. Cut into wedges. Serve with yogurt, avocado, or mild salsa. Keep spice mild for toddlers.

## Baby Adaptations
- Smash beans; cut small wedges
- Dairy and wheat allergens—introduce per your plan
