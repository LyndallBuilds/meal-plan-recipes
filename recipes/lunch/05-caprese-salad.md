## Ingredients
- 1 ripe tomato, sliced or wedged
- 2–3 oz fresh mozzarella, torn or cubed
- A few fresh basil leaves, torn (or a light drizzle of olive oil + pinch of dried basil)
- 1–2 teaspoons olive oil
- Pinch of salt and pepper for older kids/adults (optional)
- Optional side: soft fruit (peach, nectarine, or berries)

## Instructions
1. Arrange tomato and mozzarella on a plate.
2. Tear basil over the top (or use dried basil). Drizzle olive oil.
3. Season lightly for adults; keep unsalted for babies.
4. Serve fruit on the side if you like.

## Helper Prep Tasks
- [ ] Slice tomato and portion mozzarella (same day)

## Baby Adaptations
- Dairy allergen: introduce per your plan
- Cut mozzarella and tomato into soft, manageable pieces; skip salt
