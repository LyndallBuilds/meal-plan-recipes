## Ingredients
- 4 small whole wheat tortillas
- 4–6 thin slices or shreds of mild cheese (e.g. Monterey Jack or low-moisture mozzarella)
- 4–6 thin slices of roasted or deli turkey
- ½ small apple, peeled and grated or very thinly sliced
- 1–2 teaspoons unsalted butter or neutral oil, for the pan

## Instructions
1. Lay tortillas flat. On half of each, layer a thin cover of cheese, then turkey, then apple, then a little more cheese. Fold to close.
2. Warm a skillet over medium. Melt a small amount of butter or oil. Cook each quesadilla 2–3 minutes per side until golden and the cheese is melted. Cool slightly.
3. Cut into wedges or thin strips. Serve with cucumber sticks, yogurt, or a soft vegetable your family likes.

## Helper Prep Tasks

## Baby Adaptations
- Cut into long strips for baby-led weaning; ensure apple is very soft
- Use low-sodium turkey; cheese contains dairy
- Check temperature; filling can stay hot in the center
