## Ingredients
- 2 red apples, cored and chopped
- 2 celery stalks, diced small
- 2 cups diced cooked chicken breast
- ¼ cup plain nonfat Greek yogurt
- ¼ cup mayonnaise (or more yogurt for lighter)
- ½ cup raisins (optional; chop for toddlers)
- Pinch salt and pepper for adults only (optional)
- Lettuce leaves or soft bread for serving

## Instructions
1. Chop apples and celery. Combine with chicken, yogurt, mayonnaise, and optional raisins in a bowl. Stir gently.
2. Season adult portions at the table. Serve on lettuce cups, in pita, or with crackers.
3. For younger kids, dice apples very small and chop raisins.

## Baby Adaptations
- Soft-cook apple if needed; finely dice
- Dairy and egg (mayo) allergens—introduce per your plan
