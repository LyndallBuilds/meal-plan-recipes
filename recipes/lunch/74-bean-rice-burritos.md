## Ingredients
- 2 cups cooked rice (brown or white; leftover rice works well)
- 1 small onion, finely chopped (optional/mild)
- 1 can (15 oz) low-sodium kidney beans, drained (about 2 cups)
- 8 medium flour tortillas
- ½ cup salsa
- ½ cup shredded cheese

## Instructions
1. Heat oven to 300°F. Mix rice, optional onion, and beans in a bowl.
2. Place ~½ cup filling in the center of each tortilla; fold sides in to make burritos. Arrange in a baking pan.
3. Bake 15 minutes. Pour salsa over burritos, sprinkle cheese, and serve warm.
4. For toddlers, unwrap and serve deconstructed (rice, beans, soft tortilla strips).

## Baby Adaptations
- Mash beans; skip salsa salt/spice for babies
- Wheat and dairy allergens—introduce per your plan
