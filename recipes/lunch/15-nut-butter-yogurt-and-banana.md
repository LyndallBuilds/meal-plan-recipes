## Ingredients
- ½–¾ cup plain unsweetened yogurt (Greek or regular)
- 1 tablespoon smooth nut or seed butter (peanut, almond, or sunflower — only if already introduced)
- ½ ripe banana, sliced or cut into spears
- Optional: toast, rice cake, or hemp seeds

## Instructions
1. Whisk yogurt and nut/seed butter until smooth.
2. Serve banana alongside or stirred in.
3. Add optional toast or a sprinkle of hemp seeds.

## Helper Prep Tasks
- [ ] Mix yogurt + nut butter ahead (2–3 days in fridge)

## Baby Adaptations
- Dairy and nut/seed allergens: introduce per your plan
- Keep nut butter thinned into yogurt so it is not sticky
