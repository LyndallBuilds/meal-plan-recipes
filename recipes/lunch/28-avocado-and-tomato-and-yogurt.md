## Ingredients
- ½ ripe avocado, sliced or cubed
- ½ ripe tomato, sliced or wedged (or a handful of cherry tomatoes)
- 1 single-serve plain yogurt cup or pouch (no added sugar), or ½ cup yogurt in a container
- Optional: hemp seeds; nori snacks for older toddlers

## Instructions
1. Cut avocado and tomato into soft, easy-to-pick-up pieces.
2. Plate with yogurt. Sprinkle hemp seeds if using.
3. Pack with an ice pack for lunchboxes; keep cold until eating.

## Helper Prep Tasks
- [ ] Portion yogurt cups
- [ ] Cut produce same morning for best texture

## Baby Adaptations
- Dairy allergen if using dairy yogurt: introduce per your plan
- Soft ripe avocado and tomato work well as finger food
