## Ingredients
- 1 can (15 oz) no-salt-added chickpeas, drained and rinsed
- 1 small cucumber, diced small
- 1 cup halved cherry tomatoes, seeds removed for younger kids if you prefer
- ¼ cup crumbled feta (optional; omit for under 12 months or use unsalted)
- 2 tablespoons extra-virgin olive oil
- 1 tablespoon lemon juice
- 1 small handful chopped fresh parsley
- ½ teaspoon dried oregano (optional, very mild)
- 2–3 small whole wheat pitas, warmed and cut into wedges, or thin rice cakes

## Instructions
1. Pat chickpeas dry. Gently mash about half with a fork in a large bowl, leaving the rest whole for texture.
2. Add cucumber, tomato, optional feta, olive oil, lemon juice, parsley, and oregano. Stir gently. Season to taste for adults; keep a plain portion for babies if needed.
3. Serve with pitas for scooping or deconstruct for younger eaters (chickpeas, cukes, and bread separately).

## Helper Prep Tasks
- [ ] Drain and rinse chickpeas; mash half in a container
- [ ] Dice cucumber and prep tomatoes; store

## Baby Adaptations
- Smash chickpeas fully for gummers; watch tomato skin
- Feta and wheat (pita) are allergens—serve according to your introduction plan
