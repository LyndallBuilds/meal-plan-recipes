## Ingredients
- 1 can (5 oz) tuna in water, drained
- 1 tablespoon light mayonnaise (or plain yogurt)
- 4 flour tortillas (8-inch), or smaller soft tortillas for kids
- ½ cup shredded reduced-fat cheddar

## Instructions
1. Mix tuna and mayonnaise in a small bowl.
2. **Microwave:** Spoon filling onto half of each tortilla, top with cheese, fold. Microwave on high about 60 seconds, rotating halfway.
3. **Or stovetop:** Divide mixture onto two tortillas, add cheese, top with remaining tortillas. Cook in a lightly oiled skillet until browned on both sides and cheese melts. Cut into wedges.

## Baby Adaptations
- Use soft tortillas cut into strips; mash tuna finely
- Fish, egg (mayo), wheat, and dairy allergens—introduce per your plan
