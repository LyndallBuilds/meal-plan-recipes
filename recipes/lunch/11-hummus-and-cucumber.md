## Ingredients
- ½–¾ cup hummus (store-bought short-ingredient, or homemade)
- 1 small cucumber, cut into spears or sticks
- Optional: toast strips or pita for dipping
- Optional: a few cubes of mild cheese
- Optional: pinch of sesame seeds or paprika on the hummus

## Instructions
1. Spoon hummus into a small bowl or sectioned plate.
2. Cut cucumber into spears (peel optional).
3. Add optional toast/pita and cheese. Sprinkle paprika or sesame on hummus if using.
4. Serve as a dip plate — cucumber and toast for scooping.

## Helper Prep Tasks
- [ ] Cut cucumber spears (keep in water in the fridge up to a few days)
- [ ] Portion hummus into small containers

## Baby Adaptations
- Sesame (tahini in hummus) and optional wheat/dairy: introduce per your plan
- Thin thick hummus with a little water or olive oil for easier scooping
- Offer soft cucumber sticks without peel if peel is tough
