## Ingredients
- 3 eggs
- ½ ripe avocado (use more if you want it creamier)
- Pinch of onion powder or pepper (optional)
- Optional: sesame seeds on top
- Optional side: tomato wedges or toast

## Instructions
1. Hard-boil eggs (about 10–12 minutes), cool, and peel.
2. Mash eggs with avocado until creamy with a little texture. Season lightly if desired.
3. Scoop into bowls or onto toast. Add optional seeds and tomato on the side.

## Helper Prep Tasks
- [ ] Boil eggs on a prep day (store in shell up to 1 week)
- [ ] Mash with avocado close to serving time

## Baby Adaptations
- Egg (and sesame if used) allergens: introduce per your plan
- Mash smooth for babies; offer as a spread on soft toast strips for toddlers
