## Ingredients
- 1 small zucchini, finely grated and squeezed to remove extra moisture
- 1 large egg
- ⅓ cup whole milk
- 2 tablespoons melted unsalted butter, cooled
- ¾ cup whole wheat flour
- 1 teaspoon baking powder
- ¼ teaspoon salt
- ½–¾ cup freshly grated mild cheddar, divided
- 2 tablespoons finely grated Parmesan (optional)

## Instructions
1. Preheat oven to 375°F. Grease a 24-cup mini muffin pan well, or use silicone.
2. Whisk egg, milk, and butter. Stir in squeezed zucchini. In another bowl, mix flour, baking powder, and salt. Combine wet and dry with a few stirs, then fold in most of the cheddar and optional Parmesan. Do not overmix.
3. Fill cups almost full. Sprinkle remaining cheese on top.
4. Bake 14–16 minutes until tops spring back and a pick comes out with dry crumbs. Cool a few minutes in the pan, then on a rack.

## Baby Adaptations
- Great finger food for 9+ months when dairy and wheat are safe for your family
- Mini size; split for younger babies
- Low salt; add salt to adult portions on the side if needed
