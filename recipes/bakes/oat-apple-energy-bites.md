## Ingredients
- 1 cup rolled oats
- ½ cup natural smooth almond butter or sunflower seed butter
- ¼–⅓ cup finely grated apple, squeezed to remove extra juice
- 2–3 tablespoons ground flaxseed or a little extra oat flour
- ½ teaspoon ground cinnamon
- 1–2 teaspoons maple syrup (optional; omit for under 1 year)
- 1–2 tablespoons mini chocolate chips (optional, for older children only)

## Instructions
1. In a medium bowl, combine oats, nut or seed butter, apple, flax, and cinnamon. Add optional maple a little at a time if the mixture is too dry.
2. Squeeze a small amount to form a 1–1½ inch ball; if it won't hold, add a few drops of water or a tiny more seed butter, not so much that it is sticky to handle.
3. Roll into 16–20 small balls. If using chips, press one or two into a few of the larger kids’ portion only, not the babies’.
4. Chill 30+ minutes. Store in the fridge 5–7 days in a sealed container, layers separated with parchment if needed.

## Helper Prep Tasks
- [ ] Grate and squeeze apple
- [ ] Portion and roll balls onto a lined tray, cover, and refrigerate

## Baby Adaptations
- Omit sweetener and chocolate for infants
- Allergen: nut or seed butter—per your plan
- Soft, squishable; supervise eating
